CNN.IBC.com - February 8, 2001

The Imperial Bureau of Astonomical Affairs confirms Earth's orbit to be moved in the next year The Emperor has announced today funds have been held for Earth's orbit to be moved 175,000 miles from the Sun in the next 15 months. However, His Grandness has requested the city of Shefferville to remain at its actual orbital position, hence the construction of a new moon being scheduled for completion by early 2002. It is said the new moon would be named after Her Majesty's daughther Princess Candy. The City of Shefferville and its inhabitants are planned to be moved to Moon Candy in the second quarter of 2002.

CNN.IBC.com - February 8, 2001

Following imperial's annoucement of a new moon construction to be undertaken next year, an outstanding sister project has been revealed. The Space & Cosmos High Rise Co. has been awarded a $159.8 trillion contract for a fixed standing elevator to be built and installed between Moon Candy and mainland Earth. Projected elevator specifications are astonishing as well. 175,000 miles-long and 100 feet-wide, the hyper-structure will allow 200,000 people to travel safely in a comfortable environment in 3 days while enjoying all commodities such a journey will call for, namely food and beverage services, rest and sleep areas along with personal showers, personal TV screens and shopping accomodations.

The elevator will be installed by self-automated flying devices capable of travelling in both athmosphere and airless altitudes.

Quite surprisingly, no information are available at this time regarding air supply and emergency facilities. According, to the Space & Cosmos High Rise Co. CEO, M. Paul Martin, all such technicial issues will be addressed in mid-term design phases.

Press Release - February 9, 2001

The Space & Cosmos High Rise Co. would like to announce the nomination of Mr.Wayne Gretzky as the new CEO. Mr. Gertzky declared in his nomination press conference he will do everything he can to find the former CEO Mr. Martin wherever he’s hiding with the 50 trillion dollars he allegedly stole from the Imperial Space elevator project. Mr. Gretzky would like to reassure the shareholders and the Imperial authorities that the shortage of money caused by the Martin incident will not compromise the project in any ways whatsoever. History has proven that a compnay may survive very well despise a 75% market value plunge. In his speech, Mr.Gretzky rejected all the accusation regarding his lack of knowledge in the space business or engineering. “ Space is like hockey. With the right team you can win. I’m a winner.” said Mr. Gretzky when a reporter asked him if his nomination was a publicity stunt. When asked bout the consequence of the Martin incident, the emperor declared “Des trillions, des millions. Que voulez-vous, c’est dur à compter avec juste dix doigts. Moé itou j’aurais pus me tromper .” So far, the Imperial government do not intend to press charge against Mr.Martin.
